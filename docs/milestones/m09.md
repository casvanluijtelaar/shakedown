# M9 Invalid, not failed: report

M9 is complete, at version `0.0.10`. Exit criteria 1 to 4 are met, with the Rust jobs of GitHub Actions failing as criterion 4 says. The Shadow stream cut of `SPK-15` and `US-059` was not driven. The owner waived the demo of criterion 5, and it was not run.

## What it built

| Part | Where | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| The heartbeat and the clock watch | `addon/addons/main/functions/fn_init.sqf`, `crates/shakedown/src/runner/session.rs` | `HLT-02`: from `ready` on, each machine sends its clocks once a second from an `EachFrame` handler. The runner flags a machine whose mission time runs under half of real time, or whose frame number stands still, over 5 s, and clears the flag after 5 s of moving clocks. The flag is in `status` (`INS-04`). A check during which a machine is flagged ends `invalid` / `unhealthy`. |
| Lost machines | `runner/session.rs`, `runner/run.rs`, `runner/control.rs` | `P-07`, `OBS-03`, `P-09`: a watch of every process handle and heartbeat. A lost machine breaks the session and ends the check at once, and the next checks are `session-broken`. A crash's `.mdmp` and `.bidmp` are copied into the run folder (`RES-05`). |
| The order of causes | `runner/run.rs`, `crates/shakedown-protocol/src/result.rs` | `DM-06`: the first cause decides between `fail`, `machine-lost` and `unhealthy`, each with its time (`RES-05`). |
| Asking a broken session | `runner/control.rs` | `INS-01`, `INS-03`: `inspect` and `exec` of a lost machine answer at once with its state; `inspect`, `logs` and `errors` of the machines that still answer work on a broken session. `inspect` of a machine that runs no check names the lead's step whose order runs there (`m09-1`). |
| The fixture's failures on demand | `fixtures/skdfx-ext/`, `fixtures/skd_fixture/` | `FIX-01`, `FIX-02`: the extension `skdfx_x64.dll`, `skdfx_fnc_crash` (an access violation in native code, with a watchdog that ends the process 5 s after the fault if the engine's crash handling leaves it running), `skdfx_fnc_exit` and `skdfx_fnc_holdClock` (`setAccTime 0`). `tools/install.py` builds it. |

## The spikes

[Research 28](../../research/28-spikes-health.md) holds all three, run from `crates/shakedown/tests/spikes_m09.rs`.

- **`SPK-16`**: the fixture's crash ended a singleplayer `p1`, a dedicated server and a client 5 of 5 each, with both dumps. A headless client sometimes stayed up for 61 s after writing its dumps. Rather than take the fallback, which replaces the engine's dumps with dumps the harness writes, the extension keeps the real fault and adds the 5 s watchdog. With it every kind ended within 10 s, 5 of 5, each with both dumps.
- **`SPK-17`** passed: `setAccTime 0` held singleplayer mission time under 0.04 s over 8 s while frames, heartbeats and an `exec` went on, 10 of 10. `FIX-02` stays.
- **`SPK-15`** passed: over 50 passing checks in singleplayer and 50 on a dedicated session with two clients and a headless client, no heartbeat gap was above 1.12 s and no 5 s window of mission time under 0.995 of real time. A suspended client was `lost` in 9.3 to 10.1 s and a killed one in 0.4 to 0.6 s, 5 of 5. The clock watch changes outcomes, so the fallback is not needed. The Shadow stream cut that research 17 reported was not driven, as nobody could cut the owner's stream during the spike; `SPK-17`'s held clock reproduces what that research measured.

## Decisions written into the spec

- `HLT-02`: the thresholds hold, when a flag clears and from when a clock counts as stopped, and the 10 s heartbeat gap, counted only while the runner runs (`m09-2`). `P-07` takes the gap as its third way to see a stall.
- `FIX-01` and the fixture contract: what the engine does on each kind of process, and the watchdog that ends a process its crash handling leaves running 5 s after the fault. `FIX-02`: the clock is held with `setAccTime 0`.
- `P-09`: the dumps are the `.mdmp` and `.bidmp` in the profile folder; a process that ends without the runner having asked is `exited`, whatever its code.
- `INS-01`: `inspect` of another machine gives the step whose wait, poll or code runs there, else `[]` (`m09-1`).
- The domain model: a machine is `lost` also when its heartbeat does not come for 10 s, and `machine-lost` names that reason.

## The demo

The milestone's demo: during a run on a dedicated session with two clients, `p2`'s process is suspended, and in a second session ended. Each time the check is `invalid` naming `p2`, the checks after it are `session-broken`, `inspect` returns `p2` `lost` within 20 s, and the result file holds `p2`'s log up to its end and any crash dump.

The owner waived this demo on 2026-11-13, and it was not run. The acceptance round's `US-015`, `US-016`, `US-043` and `US-045` checks drove the same suspension, end and crash of `p2`.

## Acceptance

The test developer's rounds wrote three findings, for the developer, all answered and verified:

- `m09-1`: `inspect` of a client running a check's wait named the check but no step. It names the step whose order runs there; 3 of 3.
- `m09-2`: a runner suspended about 25 s and resumed marked every machine `lost` and broke the session. The heartbeat gap counts only time the runner runs; 3 of 3.
- `m09-3`: in one full run the server logged `Object 2:89 not found` once in 78 executions, in the reset after a check killed `p2`. The developer deferred it as `DEF-25`, as no promise covers a dead player's body, and changed no framework code. The tester verified the deferral and made its `DEF-09` check tell a killed body's line apart, 12 of 12.

The M9 round, against the framework from `2ab3b28`, ran the 34 M9 checks three times, each repeat in sessions of its own: 99 of 102 passed, every check 3 of 3 but `m09-1`'s. The full run, against the framework from `ba0ae21`, passed 271 of 277, every M9 check among them. Five failures were starts lost to `Error 20 reading file` of a base-game PBO, the test machine; all five passed when run again. The sixth was `m09-3`. The runs are in [the M9 coverage](../../acceptance/coverage/m09.md).

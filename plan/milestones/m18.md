# M18 The full health watch

## Goal

Finish the health watch of `CR-30`. Its clock watch (`HLT-02`) has held since M9: in M10 it ended the checks of one slow `sp-cba-ace` session `invalid` rather than `fail` (research 29). No run of the port met a struggling machine the clock watch missed, so this milestone comes last of phase 2's features, before the release (M19), and moves up only when one does.

## Demo

On a dedicated session with two clients, `p2` starved of CPU during an arma-grappling-hook check makes it `invalid` / `unhealthy` naming `p2` and its frame rate, and `status` shows each machine's frame rate and answer time.

## Stories

- `US-093` (feature) See each machine's frame rate and answer time
- `US-094` (system fail) A machine's frame rate collapses or its answers lag during a check

## What it builds

- The frame rate and answer time in the heartbeat and in `INS-04`, their thresholds from `SPK-22`, and `unhealthy` for them in `DM-05`.
- `docs/`: the health watch.

## Spikes

- **`SPK-22`**, whole, runs before the thresholds; its pass condition is in `spec/06-open-questions.md`. If it fails: frame rate and answer time are shown in `status` and flag no check, and `US-094` is edited to say so.

## Exit criteria

1. `US-093` and `US-094` pass their acceptance criteria on the test machine.
2. The spikes' results are in `research/`, and the spec is edited in place to what they found.
3. Every check and test it adds carries the story or requirement ID it proves (`NFR-MT-02`), the gates of `NFR-MT-03` pass, and `docs/` say how to use what it built.
4. The owner has run the demo on the Windows test machine.

## Left out

- The process and window checks `HLT-01` also names: a lost process is caught by `P-09`, and a window is needed only by a hand over.

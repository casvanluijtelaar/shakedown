# M7 Reload inside the mission

## Goal

Give a mod whose functions are not final the edit-and-check loop of G2: under 30 s from a reload to a check's verdict, with no restart, in singleplayer and on a dedicated server.

## Demo

With a dedicated session of two clients, the fixture's `fn_version.sqf` changed from `"1"` to `"2"` is reloaded with level `mission`, and a check tagged with a finding sees `"2"` on every machine, under 30 s from the reload to its outcome.

## Stories

- `US-054` (feature) Reload a changed function inside the mission and rerun the checks a finding names
- `US-058` (mistake) A reload asked while a run is active

## What it builds

- Level A of `P-08`: `BIS_fnc_recompile` on every machine, verified by the hash of each function's source.
- `reload: partial` on the checks after it (`RLD-05`, `DM-08`).
- A reload or reset asked during a run, reload or reset is refused with `session-busy` (`RLD-01`).
- `docs/`: which changes reload inside the mission, and what `partial` means.

## Spikes

- **`SPK-09`**, whole, runs before the work that depends on it; its pass condition is in `spec/06-open-questions.md`. If it fails: Every `.sqf` change is level `mission-restart`, and `RLD-02` loses its level-A row for mod code. That changes the promise: `US-054` cannot be met as written. The milestone then edits `RLD-02`, `US-054` and `NFR-PF-01` to say a mod change reloads by a mission restart, `RLD-05` and `DM-08` become moot, and the owner confirms the edit before the milestone closes.

## Exit criteria

1. `US-054` and `US-058` pass their acceptance criteria on the test machine.
2. `NFR-PF-01` holds in singleplayer and on a dedicated session with two clients.
3. The spike's result is in `research/`, and the spec is edited in place to what it found.
4. Every check and test it adds carries the story or requirement ID it proves (`NFR-MT-02`), the gates of `NFR-MT-03` pass, and `docs/` say how to use what it built.
5. The owner has run the demo on the Windows test machine.

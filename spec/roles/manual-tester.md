# Manual tester

**Who.** A person, never an AI agent, who judges what a check cannot: how a thing looks, sounds and feels in the game (PRD section 3). The owner does this for the projects Shakedown serves.

**Trying to get done.** Be put at the start of a situation set up by the framework, with a list of what to look for, instead of pasting into the debug console.

**What they understand.** The game as a player does, and the mod's user stories. Not SQF, not the framework: a situation is described to them in what they will see and where to look.

The manual tester's requirement, `MAN-01` (`CR-52`), is a `should`. Their stories are written when a milestone after the MVP takes it up.

# Manual tester stories

* [US-083 Be put at the start of a situation with a list of what to look for](US-083-handed-a-situation.md) - A manual tester is put in a client's window at the start of a situation a check built, with the list of what to look for on screen and in the command's output, and the session stays up until they are done.

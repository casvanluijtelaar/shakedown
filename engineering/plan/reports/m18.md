# M18 The full health watch: report

M18 is complete, at version `0.1.8`, and with it the features of phase 2. Exit criteria 1 to 3 are met. On 2026-11-23 the owner waived the demo of criterion 4, and it was not run.

The M18 build set the workspace version to `0.1.8`. M18 closes after M19's build, which raised the workspace version ahead for M19's pre-release, so the binaries built at the close commit report `0.2.0-rc.1`, not `0.1.8`.

## What it built

| Part | Where | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Frame rate and answer time | `crates/shakedown/`, `addon/` | `HLT-01`: each machine's frame rate from the heartbeat's frame numbers, and its answer time from a ping the runner sends with each heartbeat, answered through the path of every order. |
| The watch | `crates/shakedown/` | `HLT-01`, `DM-05`, `DM-06`: a machine under 10 frames a second, or answering slower than 2 s, over 5 s of real time is flagged, and a check that overlaps the flag ends `invalid` / `unhealthy` naming the machine and the measure, unless a cause for `fail` came first. |
| `status` | `crates/shakedown/`, `engineering/spec/contract/cli.md`, `engineering/spec/contract/mcp-tools.md` | `INS-04`, `US-093`: a `health` per machine with its flag, frame rate, answer time and clocks, a health line under each machine in text, and the same keys from the `status` tool. |
| The docs | `docs/outcomes-and-broken-sessions.md` | The health watch. |

## The spike

`SPK-22` passed ([research 36](../../research/36-spike-frame-rate-and-answer-time.md)): over 50 passing checks in singleplayer and 50 on a dedicated session no healthy machine came under 44 frames a second or above 0.03 s, and a client suspended 480 ms of every 500 ms was flagged by its frame rate in 3.6 to 5.6 s, 5 of 5. The floor is 10 frames a second and the ceiling 2 s; `HLT-01`, `INS-04`, `DM-05`, the domain model and `US-093` were edited in place to it.

## Tests

With the game, after `python tools/install.py`: `cargo test -p shakedown --test m18 -- --ignored --nocapture --test-threads=1`, with `us_093_us_094_hlt_01_frame_rate_and_answer_time_on_a_dedicated_session`, and the spike in `crates/shakedown/tests/spikes_m18.rs`. The watch's logic has unit tests that need no game.

No repeated-demo test was run at the close: no exit criterion of M18 asks for one apart from the demo the owner waived.

Two of them, `US-015` checks of M9, failed once because the watch ended a check `invalid` / `unhealthy` on `p1`'s answer time while the check waited for `p2` to be lost; both outcomes are `invalid`, and both passed on their rerun.

## Decisions written into the spec

- `HLT-01`: a frame rate floor of 10 frames a second and an answer time ceiling of 2 s, each over 5 s of real time, beside the clock watch of `HLT-02`; mission time that falls behind while the frame rate is under its floor is named by the frame rate.
- `INS-04`, `DM-05`, `engineering/spec/contract/cli.md` and `engineering/spec/contract/mcp-tools.md`: the `health` of each machine in `status`, and `unhealthy` for the frame rate and the answer time.

## The demo

The milestone's demo: on a dedicated session with two clients, `p2` starved of CPU during an arma-grappling-hook check, with `status` showing each machine's frame rate and answer time. The owner waived it on 2026-11-23, and it was not run. The acceptance round drove the same starving during a fixture check.

## Acceptance

The M18 round, on 2026-11-22 at `63f81bc`, passed 4 of 4 and the 45 earlier checks, with no finding.

## Left out

- The process and window checks `HLT-01` also names: a lost process is caught by `P-09`, and a window is needed only by a hand over.
- Not driven by the round: the `answer-time` flag, which starving a process does not raise before the frame rate, and `inspect` of the frame rate.
- Starting game clients minimised and never active (`DEF-34`, open, no milestone yet).

# M15 Hand over to the manual tester: report

## Decisions written into the spec

- `MAN-01` and `engineering/spec/contract/check-api.md`: `skd_fnc_handOver [machine, items]`, with `items` an array of one to ten strings that are not empty, written for a person who knows the game and not SQF. It does not return: the body ends there, every step still running closes as `passed`, and the outcome is fixed as for a body that reached its end, so an earlier script error still ends the check `fail` / `script-error`. A check that ends `pass` is handed over; one that ends otherwise is not, and the run goes on with its reset.
- `MAN-01`: the box stands at the top centre, in the column Arma 3's default HUD layout leaves free between the vehicle info and the weapon info, in the game's GUI grid units from 11.5 units off the safe zone's left edge to 15.3 off its right, at most 24 wide, so it covers neither the action menu and communication menu nor the weapon info, stance, stamina bar and hints, at any aspect ratio or interface size.
- `engineering/spec/contract/check-api.md`: the failures of `skd_fnc_handOver` are `fail` / `check-error` for `server` or a headless client and for items of the wrong form, and `fail` / `wait-ceiling` when the list is not on the client's screen within 10 s. In singleplayer `"server"` names `p1`.
- `RUN-04`, `STP-02`, `RUN-07`: a run that hands over ends there, its session stays up, and the next `run`, `reset`, `reload` or `session stop` takes it back.

## The demo

`cd e2e; ..\target\shakedown\shakedown.exe run --setup dedicated handover/US-083-crate` builds the scene around the fixture's crate in a dedicated session and hands over to `p1`; the owner looks through `p1`'s window over Shadow PC.

The owner's first look, on 2026-11-19, found two defects, which the developer raised as findings:

- `m15-1`, the look-for box covered the action menu: it stood at the top left, over the game's scroll menu, so the owner could not see which action they selected. The box was moved into the free top-centre column above, and `MAN-01`, the check API and `docs/handing-over.md` say where it stands.
- `m15-2`, the demo's crate offered no `Open crate` and gave no first aid kit. The framework and the fixture were right; the demo check stood `p1` 3.01 m from the crate, past the action's 3 m radius, on the clients' shared start spot, with a first aid kit already in its loadout. The check now stands `p1` 2 m from the crate away from the start spot, takes its kit out, waits on `p1` for the action in reach and lists items that hold, and `docs/handing-over.md` says how.

Both were answered and verified by the tester. At the owner's second look the same day, everything worked as expected.

## Acceptance

The first M15 round, on 2026-11-18, passed its 14 checks 42 of 42. The verify round, on 2026-11-19 at `e76eaa6`, added the checks of `m15-1` and `m15-2` and passed 48 of 48, each check 3 of 3, with no new finding. It noted that in `run --each-setup` the checks of the setups a hand over keeps from starting appear in no result file and no count, which the contract does not promise either way.

## Left out

- `shakedown status` shows the session a hand over left up, but not that it was handed over.
- A reload at level `none`, which changes nothing in the game, leaves the box on screen; the next reset removes it.
- A HUD layout the player changed in the game's options is not followed: the box's place is worked out from the default layout.
- One client at a time: a hand over goes to one game client, not to several at once.
- No verdict from the manual tester is recorded in the result file: the owner reports what they saw as a finding or in the milestone report.

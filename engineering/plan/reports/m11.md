# M11 Late joiners: report

M11 is complete, at version `0.1.1`. Exit criteria 1, 3 and 4 are met. On 2026-11-15 the owner left criterion 2, the late-joiner cases of arma-grappling-hook's `US-009-1-freed` and `MP-01-drawn`, to that repository's roles, and M11 does not wait on it. On 2026-11-23 the owner waived the demo of criterion 5, and it was not run.

The M11 build set the workspace version to `0.1.1`. M11 closes after M15 to M19's builds, which raised the workspace version ahead for M19's pre-release, so the binaries built at the close commit report `0.2.0-rc.1`, not `0.1.1`.

## What it built

| Part | Where | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Late clients | `crates/shakedown/`, `spec/contract/cli.md` | `MCH-01`, `CFG-01`: the setup key `late` names clients that the session does not start; `session start`, `status` and `skd_fnc_machines` leave them out until a check joins them. |
| Join and leave | `addon/`, `crates/shakedown/`, `spec/contract/check-api.md` | `skd_fnc_join` starts a late client or one that left, and returns once it is `ready`, within its ceiling of 180 s by default; `skd_fnc_leave` kicks a client by its profile name and returns once the server's `PlayerDisconnected` fired, within 20 s by default. Each is a step of the check in the result file. |
| A join that fails | `crates/shakedown/` | `US-068`: a client that is not ready within the join's ceiling, or exits during its join, ends the check `invalid` / `machine-lost`, naming it with its last RPT lines or exit code, and the session goes on without it. |
| Joins and leaves that cannot be | `addon/` | `US-069`, `DM-04`: a join or leave of `server`, a headless client, a machine already joined or left, an unknown machine or in singleplayer ends the check `fail` / `check-error`, naming the machines that can join and leave then. |
| The restore | `crates/shakedown/`, `fn_reset.sqf` | `RST-01`, `P-06`: before the reset in place the session is brought back to the machines it started with, ending late clients that joined and joining clients that left; the next reset deletes the unit a client left behind (`m12-1`). |
| The fixture's record | `fixtures/`, `spec/contract/fixture-mod.md` | The server's `PlayerDisconnected` handler adds the machine to `skdfx_disconnected`. |
| The docs | `docs/dedicated-sessions.md`, `docs/check-api.md`, `docs/project-file.md`, `docs/porting-a-suite.md` | Joining late, leaving and joining again, the `late` key, and late clients in a ported suite. |

## Decisions written into the spec

- `MCH-01` and `spec/contract/check-api.md`: `skd_fnc_join [machine, ceiling]` and `skd_fnc_leave [machine, ceiling]`, run from a check on a dedicated session; a leave is a kick by profile name, seen through the server's `PlayerDisconnected`.
- `CFG-01` and `spec/contract/cli.md`: the setup key `late`, a count of clients the session does not start.
- `P-02`, `P-06`, `RST-01`: the session starts without its late clients, and the reset first restores the machines the session started with; the unit a client left behind stays as a body until the next reset deletes it.
- `spec/contract/result-file.md`: each start of a machine, joined and left ones too, with its own start number, and joins and leaves as steps.

## The demo

The milestone's demo: arma-grappling-hook's `US-009-1-freed` and `MP-01-drawn` with their late-joiner cases on `dedicated-cba` with `late = 1`. The owner waived it on 2026-11-23, and it was not run. The acceptance rounds drove the same join and leave on the fixtures.

## Acceptance

The M11 round, on 2026-11-15 at `15df612`, passed 67 of 67 and found no defect. The M12 round, on 2026-11-16, saw in one of four runs of M11's dedicated part the reset after a rejoin fail on `p2`'s position, and raised `m12-1`: the unit a client left behind stayed at its start position, and each rejoin put the new unit among those bodies. The developer made the next reset delete it, and the tester verified it, 51 of 51 with `--repeat 3`.

## Left out

- Restarting the server during a session (`DEF-31`, no milestone yet).
- Late and leaving headless clients, and a join or leave through a tool outside a check.
- Parts of `MCH-01` no story criterion names, listed by the M11 round and not deferred: the restore run by `shakedown reset`, the restore before a `@reset mission` check, a restore that does not complete, a leave the server does not take from the kick, and a client joined by the last check of a run, which `status` lists until the next check's reset.

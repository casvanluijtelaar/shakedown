# M15 Hand over to the manual tester

## Goal

Make the owner's look in the game cheap. The demos of M6 to M10 were waived, each a situation to set up by hand; arma-grappling-hook's spikes and demos end in the owner looking at a rope from a client (its SPK-01 and SPK-02 pass conditions, its M01 demo); and this project's working rules leave the look and feel to the owner, with the session setting up the situation. A hand over makes that one command.

## Demo

A check of this repository's `e2e` project builds a scene around the fixture's crate in a dedicated session and hands over to `p1`; the owner, in `p1`'s window, finds the three things to look for on screen and in the command's output, and the session stays up until `session stop`. arma-grappling-hook's check that builds a rope on the castle tower, puts `p1` on it 10 m up and hands over is the same command in that repository, once its roles write the check.

## Stories

- `US-083` (feature) Be put at the start of a situation with a list of what to look for
- `US-084` (mistake) A hand over to a machine with no screen

## What it builds

- `skd_fnc_handOver` (`MAN-01`) in the check API contract and the addon: the list on the client's screen until the next reset, and a refusal for the server and a headless client (`DM-04`).
- The `handed_over` record of a check execution and the not-run reason `handed-over` in the result file and its schema; `handed_over` in `run_result`; the machine, the items and `shakedown session stop` printed by `shakedown run`.
- A run that ends after a hand over and keeps its session up, also one that started its own (`RUN-04`, `STP-02`), and in `run --each-setup` starts no further setup (`RUN-07`).
- `e2e/`: the hand over check the owner runs, against the fixture's crate.
- `docs/`: handing over to a manual tester.

## Spikes

None.

## Exit criteria

1. `US-083` and `US-084` pass their acceptance criteria on the test machine.
2. Every check and test it adds carries the story or requirement ID it proves (`NFR-MT-02`), the gates of `NFR-MT-03` pass, and `docs/` say how to use what it built.
3. The owner has run the demo on the Windows test machine.

## Left out

- A verdict from the manual tester recorded into the result file: the owner reports what they saw as a finding or in the milestone report.
- A hand over to more than one client at once.

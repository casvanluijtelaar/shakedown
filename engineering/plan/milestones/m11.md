# M11 Late joiners

## Goal

Port what the M10 port could not: the late-joiner cases of arma-grappling-hook's dedicated checks (`DEF-04`), and the disconnects and rejoins its spec promises (`MP-02`, `CTL-03` of that repository). Until they run, the PowerShell suite cannot be retired, and its M01 suite runs every dedicated check with a late client as well (`-LateClients 1`).

## Demo

arma-grappling-hook's `US-009-1-freed` and `MP-01-drawn` run with their late-joiner cases on `dedicated-cba` with `late = 1`, the late `p3` joining after the rope is up and agreeing with the server, and reach the verdicts its PowerShell suite reaches on the same build.

## Stories

- `US-066` (feature) A client joins a dedicated session late and sees what the others see
- `US-067` (feature) A client leaves and joins again
- `US-068` (system fail) A joining client never becomes ready
- `US-069` (mistake) Joining or leaving a machine that cannot

## What it builds

- The setup key `late` and late clients that the session does not start (`MCH-01`, `CFG-01`), in the CLI contract and the project file's validation.
- `skd_fnc_join` and `skd_fnc_leave` in the check API contract and the addon, with the runner starting and ending a client mid-session and reading the server's `PlayerDisconnected` for it.
- The reset's restore of the session's machines before the reset in place (`MCH-01`, `RST-01`).
- A fixture behaviour: a server-side `PlayerDisconnected` handler that sets a public value a check reads (`spec/contract/fixture-mod.md`).
- `docs/`: joining and leaving; `docs/porting-a-suite.md` on late clients.
- In arma-grappling-hook's repository, by its own roles: the late-joiner cases of `US-009-1-freed` and `MP-01-drawn`.

## Spikes

- **`SPK-18`**, whole, runs before the work that depends on it; its pass condition is in `spec/06-open-questions.md`. If it fails: without a clean leave from inside a client, a leave ends the client's process and the generated `server.cfg` sets a disconnect timeout of 5 s, so a leave's default ceiling is 10 s; without a rejoin under the machine's name, rejoining waits, `MCH-01` and `US-067` are edited to say so, and the reset restores a client that left by a mission restart.

## Exit criteria

1. `US-066`, `US-067`, `US-068` and `US-069` pass their acceptance criteria on the test machine.
2. The late-joiner cases of arma-grappling-hook's `US-009-1-freed` and `MP-01-drawn` reach the PowerShell suite's verdicts on the same build (`DEF-04`'s acceptance criterion).
3. The spikes' results are in `research/`, and the spec is edited in place to what they found.
4. Every check and test it adds carries the story or requirement ID it proves (`NFR-MT-02`), the gates of `NFR-MT-03` pass, and `docs/` say how to use what it built.
5. The owner has run the demo on the Windows test machine.

## Left out

- Restarting the server during a session (`DEF-31`).
- Late and leaving headless clients: arma-grappling-hook's headless client runs nothing.
- A join or leave of a machine through a tool outside a check; a check is the one place a join or leave is promised.

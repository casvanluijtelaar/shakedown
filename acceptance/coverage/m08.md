## The M8 round

`python acceptance/run.py --repeat 3 --check US-033 --check US-034 --check US-036 --check US-063`, which selects the 16 checks of M8 and nothing of an earlier milestone, against the framework installed from `f6b094f`, from 09:36 to 10:01 local time on 2026-11-12: 48 of 48 passed, every M8 check 3 of 3. No part was lost to the machine; in a trial run before it, the first `run --setup dedicated-2` died on `Error 20 reading file` of `rocks_f_exp.pbo`, the machine, and the harness now starts such a part again.

Two kinds of session:

- **Its own session**, with no session running before it, once per repeat, in projects of their own: `walk` (the setup `dedicated-2`, a dedicated server with two clients loading `@skd_fixture` alone, and the three checks of `acceptance/walk/`) and `ceil` (the setup `sp` and the eight ceiling checks). Per repeat: one `run --setup dedicated-2` to its end, one stopped by Ctrl+C, one `run --setup sp` of the ceiling checks, one `session start` and `session stop`, one `session_start` and `session_stop` through the MCP server: four dedicated starts and one singleplayer start, twelve and three over the round.
- **One running singleplayer session** of the generated project (`sp`), in which the batch ran three times: three checks stopped in the second by Ctrl+C, Ctrl+Break, `shakedown run stop` and `run_stop`, and the eight ceiling checks by command and through `run_start`.

Ctrl+C and Ctrl+Break went to a process the harness started, through `harness/console_signal.py`, which runs `shakedown run` in a hidden console of its own and sends Ctrl+C to that console, or Ctrl+Break to the command's process group, as `spec/contract/cli.md` says a program does.

  1. `shakedown run --setup dedicated-2 --json` with no session started one, ran the three checks of the project, all `pass`, stopped the session and exited 0, in 73 to 82 s, the run itself 26.5 to 26.8 s. Polling `status` twice a second during it saw the session and its three game processes; afterwards no `arma3_x64` or `arma3server_x64` process ran, `status` showed `session` `null`, `netstat -ano` showed no socket on 2402 to 2406, and `MPMissions` held no copy of the session's mission.

  3. `.shakedown/.gitignore` holds `*`; `git status --porcelain --untracked-files=all` in the project was the same before and after the run, and no file outside `.shakedown/` was added or removed.
- **US-034** A run is stopped by hand:

## How NFR-US-01 was judged for a wrong argument

The M0 round left open whether the message for a wrong argument names a next step. The message is, for example, `skd_fnc_place: argument 2 (position) must be an ASL position [x, y, z] of three numbers, got STRING "here".` It names the function, the argument by number and name, what it takes and what it got, in one sentence. The rule the argument must meet is the next thing to do, so it is taken as meeting `NFR-US-01`. The other check-error messages of M1 say what to do in a sentence of their own (`Check its spelling and that its mod is in the setup.`, `Make it return true or false.`, `Add CBA to the setup's mods, or press no key in this setup.`). The check-timeout message names the step it ran long in and says to raise `@timeout` or find why the step runs long (`m01-2`).
